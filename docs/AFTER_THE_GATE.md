# After the Gate

Everything cut to make September 17, and every seventeenth design, waits
here [D12][D3]. Nothing on this list is worked before **2027-01-31** unless
Jacob moves it above the line in writing. Most of it will deserve to die
here; that is the file doing its job.

## Parked — product

- **Franklin's epitaph sheet.** His body as an old book, reissued "in a new
  and more perfect edition, corrected and amended by the Author." Written at
  twenty-two, a printer looking at death and seeing a reprint. The Christmas
  sheet someday. (Session 001.)
- **The second sixteen.** Cut from the demand map: embed and cluster the
  category's Etsy and Pinterest listings, find the underserved niches
  (proposal §VII, "demand mining"). Saratoga and Valley Forge maps are
  candidates with 250th anniversaries in late 2027.
- **A Texas timeline** for the classroom room, to complete the Texas Set the
  way the Revolution timeline completes the Founding Documents Set.
- **Framed fulfillment** beyond the local-framer flow — only if edition
  demand proves it.

## Parked — store

- Search and customer accounts. (The cart left this list in September 2026 —
  it shipped with the museum redesign, checking out through our own worker.)
- A wholesale portal. Wholesale is invoiced, not carted (proposal §III);
  Ben's relationships carry it in year one.
- Gift wrap / gift notes as a paid option.
- Digital delivery by R2-signed URLs replacing the `PARCEL_BASE_URL` scheme.

## Parked — machines

- Automated demand mining pipeline (the embedding/cluster method) as a
  `tools/` script with a written brief for the Registrar.
- A classroom-teacher email course (five founding documents, five weeks).
- Journal RSS feed.

## The rule

When something is proposed for this file's promotion, the question is still
the D3 question: *which of the sixteen does it replace* — or which number in
the ledger does it move, and by how much. Bring the number, not the mood.
