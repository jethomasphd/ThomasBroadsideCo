The collection

There are ten thousand three-dollar Declarations on the internet, and every one was made in a browser. What nobody else can sell is what this family already owns: a working press, decades of ink in Austin, and someone who checks the quotes. The Revolution was made by printers — Franklin was one — and every product page here reads the way a museum label reads: the source, the typeface, the paper, and the press it came off.
