## Film and destination

Use the landscape master for the site, LinkedIn and presentations; portrait for Reels and Stories; square for feed placements. All three tell the full 60-second story. Each format has a matching poster in `artwork/`. ## Film description / accessibility

The Heidelberg press feeds paper in Thomas Broadside Co.’s Austin shop. Contemporary reconstructions show an eighteenth-century printer, Jefferson working on a draft, and Washington beside a public reader. Real press and finishing equipment recur between the historical scenes. A sea, an olive hillside and a woodland path evoke Homer, the Beatitudes and Dante. Inked metal type leads back to the moving modern press. A quiet reading room gives way to the collection’s Declaration, Beatitudes and Odyssey broadsides. Eleanor narrates over an original chamber score and actual machinery. Closing line: “Build something that lasts.”

The film does not reenact Jesus, attribute invented speech to historical or sacred figures, or present imagined landscapes as verified biblical locations.

## Release sequence

1. Merge and deploy the film replacement. Confirm the Press-page play button opens the new 60-second film.
2. Upload the matching native video and cover. Use one of the complete captions above and its tracked link. Check crop, audio and optional captions in the channel’s preview.
3. Release the launch email to the approved audience. Parent campaign direct-mail pieces remain compatible: their links already reach the storefront.
4. Measure completed views and visits to the collection with the campaign links. No performance result is promised and no ad spend is committed by this kit.
