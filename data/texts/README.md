# The Texts

Full transcriptions behind every typeset sheet — the textual book of
record. Files use `===` to separate blocks (kicker / display / body /
close). Fetched by clerks from archives.gov, the Avalon Project, and the
standard editions; **house rule one still applies: Jacob verifies each
file against the primary source before its sheet is called cited or goes
on press [D9].** `tools/typeset.py` reads these to render `art_src/*.svg`,
`print/*.pdf`, and `site/art/*.jpg`.

The Lincoln quotes (added 2026-08-30, D3 amendment): the Second
Inaugural sheet trims the closing sentence at "the nation's wounds" —
the full sentence continues to the widow and the orphan; verify against
the Library of Congress text. The House Divided line is Lincoln quoting
Mark 3:25; the sheet credits the speech (Springfield, June 16, 1858),
verify against the Collected Works (Basler), volume 2.
