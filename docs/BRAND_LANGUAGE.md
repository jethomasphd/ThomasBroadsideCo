# Thomas Broadside Co. / Brand language

## Primary line

**Words to live with.**

This is the company line for the website colophon, campaign signatures, film closing cards and marketing correspondence. It connects the founding documents, literature and sacred texts to the rooms where people read, think, teach and live. It is the line used by the September 17 director’s-cut revision following Jacob’s correction of the prior motto.

## Supporting campaign language

- **History has a pulse.** — the living press and the flagship film.
- **Read the words. Keep the source.** — the collection’s commitment to context and citations.
- **Make room for the enduring.** — the invitation to bring the works into everyday life.
- **Before it was a relic, it was news.** — The First Impression and public words becoming public documents.

Use one principal line per composition. Preserve the distinctions among civic documents, literature and sacred texts. Source context stays close to the work.

The `motto` value in `shop.config.json` is the machine-readable company line. Templates, generated files and exported graphics must agree with it. Campaign-specific lines can complement it; they do not create additional company mottos.
