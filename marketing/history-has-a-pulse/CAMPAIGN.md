## The idea

The Declaration is the product. The press is the proof. Austin is the place.
Open with the real machine in motion, then give the viewer a simple next step:
find a broadside worth living with. Keep the same Caslon, cream, black, and red
across the screen, the inbox, and the sheet in someone's hand.

The user's September 15 commission authorizes this website and multimedia kit.
The original storefront remains the primary build; Shopify is deferred until
the visual work is ready. This campaign changes no catalog prices, source
verification, fulfillment status, ad budget, or email cadence.

## Email release

The `.eml` files are unsent drafts with the approved reply address. The HTML
uses tables, inline styles, live text, a linked still, and a functional manual
unsubscribe email. The plain-text versions carry the same destinations.
Use your email service's native unsubscribe link/list suppression if sending
a bulk campaign; replace the manual link during import. No tracking pixel is
included. Images load from `/media/press/share-card.jpg`, so deploy the website
assets before sending. Send a seed to yourself and verify the button, image,
reply, and unsubscribe path in the actual sending service. No messages have
been sent or scheduled by this work.

## Print handoff

All PDFs contain embedded fonts, a 0.125-inch bleed, and explicit TrimBox and
BleedBox. The lookbook is in reader order, ready for your pressroom to impose
as an eight-page booklet. Photo placement retains over 300 effective pixels
per inch for the enhanced portrait. Product sheets use the existing catalog
renders unchanged. Files use RGB color; they are press-layout masters, not
certified PDF/X separations. David should convert through the shop's chosen
ICC profile, proof the cream and red, and approve paper and ink. Ben supplies
the recipient addressing, postal indicia and sorting; the postcard's right
address field is intentionally open. The approved return address is included.

## What to measure

Use `links.json` as the destination map. Compare native video completion,
qualified site visits, product visits, and actual orders. Keep paid separate
from organic and read paid results against the existing growth doctrine.
The kit adds no tracking code or audience upload. On the current storefront,
the existing bell records source aggregates; Shopify measurement waits for
the later migration. Report engagement as engagement, not attributed revenue.
