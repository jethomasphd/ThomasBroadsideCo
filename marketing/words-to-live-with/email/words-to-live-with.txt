Introducing Words to Live With

A homecoming. A blessing. A question about the soul. A prayer for rest. A path through the dark.

Our Western Canon room brings together Homer, the Gospel of Matthew, Augustine, Dante, Rembrandt and Blake. Each work keeps its own voice, with its source and translation close at hand.

The new reading room includes a quiet 70-second film, narrated by Miriam, and a path into eight works from our collection. The landscapes are contemporary visual meditations; the printed sheets are the designs we make in Austin.

Read slowly. Return often. Give the words a place in your life.
