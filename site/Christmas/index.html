Choose a plate and tell Thomas Graphics a little about the door. The Austin shop will quote the covering to fit your project.

What should I measure?

Measure the face of the closed door, side to side and top to bottom, in inches. Leave out the surrounding trim. Note any windows, handles or unusual panels in the project brief.

How are material and fit decided?

The views here are design studies. The shop will confirm the material, attachment method, clearance for operation, price and timing for your door before you order.
