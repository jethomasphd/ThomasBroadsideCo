# Words to Live With

**Make room for the enduring.**

A Western Canon campaign from Thomas Broadside Co. Human owner: Jacob E. Thomas.

## The idea

A room changes when the words in it are worth returning to. This campaign invites a reader into five encounters: homecoming, mercy, the soul, rest, and the lost path. It lets the work speak, keeps the source close, and leads toward the actual broadsides made in Austin.

Homer is poetry; Matthew is scripture; Augustine addresses God in prayer; Dante writes a poem; Rembrandt and Blake interpret earlier subjects through art. The campaign keeps these distinctions visible. The contemporary landscapes create space to attend. The museum artworks and product designs remain intact.
