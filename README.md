# Thomas Broadside Co.

**Founding documents, portraits, and maps, printed on our own press in
Austin, Texas.** A venture of Thomas Graphics Inc.

> *Sell provenance, not posters.* There are ten thousand three-dollar
> Declarations on Etsy, and every one was made in a browser. What nobody
> else can sell is what we already own: a working press, a family that has
> printed in Austin for decades, and someone who will check the quotes.

This repository **is** the company's information side: the store, the
order book, the ledger, the pressroom relay, and the charters for the
reading machines that run it. Three humans own it — Jacob E. Thomas
(information), Ben Thomas (operations, the mailhouse), David Olivo
(craft, the veto) — and the machines draft while the humans sign.

## The three house rules

1. **Cited.** Every quote carries source, date, and document — on the page
   and on the sheet. No spurious Jefferson.
2. **Pre-partisan.** The store belongs to the founding era, not to a party.
3. **Printed here.** Off our press in Austin. Not print on demand. This is
   the claim no competitor can copy.

## How it works

```
                        THE INFORMATION SIDE (this repo)
   data/catalog ──build──► site/ (Cloudflare Pages, own domain, own fonts)
        ▲                     │ /api/bell (our own door counter → KV)
        │                     │ /api/counter + Stripe links (three buys, no cart)
   seven clerk charters       │ /api/stripe-webhook → order book (KV)
   in agents/*.md             ▼
                        pull_ledger.py ──► data/*.csv  (the book of record)
                              │                    │
                              ▼                    ▼
                    site/ledger (five numbers   make_job_tickets.py
                    + THE GATE, private)             │
                                                     ▼
                       THE MANUFACTURING SIDE   pressroom/RUN_SHEET.md
                       Ben & David, three       + one ticket per order,
                       buttons, big type        big type, printable
```

- **Store:** static pages generated by Python from `data/catalog/catalog.json`,
  set in Libre Caslon and IBM Plex Mono (self-hosted), cream, black, one
  red — the store looks like the sheets it sells.
- **Commerce:** Stripe payment links + one webhook. Digital downloads,
  press prints, numbered editions, invoiced wholesale. `docs/COMMERCE.md`.
- **Tracking:** a first-party bell, aggregate counts only, mirrored to CSV
  in `data/`. The dashboard at `/ledger` shows five numbers and **the
  gate: 150 orders or $5,000 by January 31, 2027** — or the press doesn't
  run again.
- **The relay:** confirmed orders become printable job tickets; the
  pressroom page is one token and three buttons (`docs/PRESSROOM_RUNBOOK.md`).
- **The clerks:** seven charters in `agents/` (Registrar, Typographer,
  Shopkeeper, Herald, Foreman, Chronicler, Keeper) under
  `agents/00-ORIENTATION.md` and the twelve rulings in
  `docs/FOUNDING_DIALOGUE.md`.

## Quickstart

```bash
python3 tools/selfcheck.py            # validate everything
python3 tools/build_site.py           # regenerate store pages from data
python3 -m http.server -d site        # preview the store
npx wrangler pages dev site           # preview with workers + local KV
```

Deploy: `docs/DEPLOY.md` (Cloudflare Pages + KV + Access, ~an afternoon).
Launch target: **September 17, 2026 — Constitution Day.**
