# Campaign copy desk

## Position

**The First Impression**

Before it was a relic, it was news.

The heritage is the work of making public words public: a draft, a press, a sheet in transit, a voice reading aloud, an argument in a newspaper. The commercial invitation is to give those words a place in your own life.

Voice: precise, warm, curious. Let the paper and the people carry the emotion. Distinguish the founders' roles. Treat the scenes as contemporary reconstructions. Use the actual collection artwork when describing a product.
