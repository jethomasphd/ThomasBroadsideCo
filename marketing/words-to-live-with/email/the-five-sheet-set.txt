The Western Canon Set

The Western Canon Set gathers five texts: The Odyssey, The Beatitudes, What is a man profited?, Our heart is restless, and Midway upon the journey.

Homer begins a story of homecoming. Matthew preserves the blessings and the question about the soul. Augustine addresses God in prayer. Dante names the dark wood at the beginning of his journey.

These works remain distinct. The source and translation accompany each sheet, so a passage can lead back to the fuller work.

Explore the set for its available formats. The three art reproductions after Rembrandt and Blake are offered separately.
