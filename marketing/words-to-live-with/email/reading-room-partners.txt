A place for lasting words.
For libraries, classrooms and reading rooms

Thomas Broadside Co. makes contemporary broadsides and art reproductions in Austin, Texas. Our Western Canon collection holds eight works drawn from Homer, Matthew, Augustine, Dante, Rembrandt and Blake.

The collection is accompanied by a new reading room: a short film, passages with their named translations, museum records, and links to each printed work.

For a library display, classroom, reading space or considered gift, write us with quantities, formats and timing. We can help you choose from the collection.

The five-sheet Western Canon Set gathers the texts. The art reproductions are available separately.
